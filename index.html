STAGE 02 · DESIGN THE INTERVENTION

Plan · Maths Talk Moves

Structure a learning experience that encourages pupils to explain, question and justify their thinking.

“What If”

Change one condition and ask pupils to predict, represent and explain what changes and what stays the same.

Example: 4 groups of 3 make 12. What if there are 5 groups instead?

“What’s Wrong”

Present a plausible mistake and ask pupils to locate the error, explain it and improve the solution.

Example: “3 × 4 = 7 because 3 + 4 = 7.” What is wrong?

Differentiated success criteria

Support (LA): use counters or diagrams to show an answer.

Core (MA): explain a pattern using a number sentence.

Challenge (HA): generalise a rule and justify whether it always works.

Teaching approach

Concrete → Pictorial → Abstract, purposeful talk, multiple representations, and formative assessment. For fractions, explicitly address equal wholes, equivalent fractions and comparing with one-half.
